Natural voice (test) - open-source parts and their licenses
- Kokoro-82M v1.0 voice model (hexgrad), Apache-2.0; run with sherpa-onnx (k2-fsa), Apache-2.0.
- misaki (hexgrad), Apache-2.0: same-spelling word sounds in misaki-het.txt (3 verb forms added by CyPress: protest, export, combat).
- CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, BSD-style license (CMUDICT-LICENSE.txt): cmu-extra.txt.
- g2p_en (Kyubyong Park), Apache-2.0: g2p-en.bin, the model that guesses words in no list.
- Apache OpenNLP, Apache-2.0: grammar tagger code. en-pos.bin was trained by CyPress on Universal Dependencies
  English Web Treebank (UD_English-EWT r2.14, Silveira et al., CC BY-SA 4.0) and English PUD (UD_English-PUD r2.14,
  CC BY-SA 3.0); the model is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Recipe: scripts/voice/TagTrain.java.
