CyPress v1.4
10 new cover styles with a visual picker, and one-tap Quick looks.
Catch up now has Brief, Edition and Week together.
CNN and Reuters work through Google News, with Bing as backup.
Per-site rules and refresh intervals, keyboard shortcuts, clearer loading and empty screens.
Fixes for cover flicker, Back button, tablet and fold covers, and pull to refresh.

1.4b fixes
- Icon picker: removed the labels under the icons
- Theme names no longer cut off (Golden hour, Arcade Cabin)
- Aurora lights now also show while reading
- One "Theme effects" setting now controls Cyber Rain glow/rain and Aurora lights
- CNN and Reuters cards no longer show the Google logo as their picture

1.5
- Theme effects for 30 themes (fireflies, embers, snow, bubbles, synthwave grid, blueprint draw-in and more)
- Pick a theme in Settings to choose its effect and whether it shows while reading
- Mosaic cover removed; Colour block, Side by side and Triptych fixed on tablets; Magazine masthead fits long names

1.6
- Settings redesigned: a full-screen list of categories, each opening its own page. Two panes on tablets, a live preview in Appearance, search that jumps to the setting
- Fewer settings: related ones merged, Motion is Full / Calm / Off, sounds and haptics removed
- Eight new cover styles: Title sequence, Chapters, Split journal, Mosaic report, Top plays, Fact panel, Collage, Italic mix
- Calmer theme effects and easier-to-read fonts across many themes; Pixel gets a little runner

1.7
- Today and Catch up are now one place: The Daily, with Front page, Brief, Edition and Week
- Add feed is simpler: a short list instead of a wall of topic chips
- New: sort feeds, folders and topics A to Z
- Dynamic theme can be light, dark, follow your phone, or follow daylight
- Fixed the oversized cover on the phone screen after folding
- Cleaner articles (no empty bullets, author pop-ups, icons or ad leftovers) and a smarter Try another way for stubborn sites like Time
- Google News stories (Goal.com and others) load faster; Pixel runner, Midnight, Back button and Settings scroll fixes

1.8
- Starter packs now add to a matching folder (Tech morning goes into Tech) and skip feeds you already have; existing split folders are merged once
- Curious mind is bigger, with science and big ideas, and there is a new Culture vulture pack
- Fixed dead feeds: Investopedia, Vulture, IGN, Serious Eats, Core77 and Cricinfo; Guardian World and It's Nice That added
- No more logos or repeated placeholder pictures on cards, and the same photo no longer shows twice in an article
- New in Settings, About: Check built-in feeds finds any that no longer load
- Mosaic report cover removed

1.9
- Settings reworded and reordered so each option says plainly what it does; the update button now says Check for updates
- More cover title styles: Glow, Gradient, Bold shadow, Spaced caps, Elegant italic (metallic removed)
- Cover masthead tidied: the edition on one line, volume and number beneath it, no ornament symbols
- Sites loaded through Google News show their own icon and more photos
- Cy runs along the top of the bottom tab bar; Dynamic look menu sits under its tile; page headers a little smaller
- Google News feeds no longer show Google's own picture on articles

1.10
- Faster start-up: the screen is drawn once for a batch of feeds instead of once per feed
- Deleting a feed now asks you to confirm first (you can still undo right after)
- Press and hold a feed in the list to rename or edit it now works on phones
- Faster grouping of the same story from different sites on The Daily
- Feed rows no longer start a drag when you press and hold on a touch screen
- Fewer saves to storage while feeds load, so the app stays responsive

1.11
- Press and hold a folder to rename it or delete it (with a confirmation); deleting a folder keeps its feeds
- Ambient theme effects pause 20 seconds after your last touch, saving battery while you read
- Feeds with a stray & or &nbsp; now load directly instead of falling back; relative links, duplicate stories and unsafe links are handled
- If phone storage is full, the app frees its cache so your settings still save, and tells you
- New: sites that publish a JSON Feed can be added like any other feed
- Feed and folder rows no longer show the text-selection menu when pressed and held

1.12
- Widget redesigned: top stories appear as photo cards with a peek at the next one, switching by themselves every few seconds or with the arrow buttons
- Read-aloud highlight now keeps pace with the voice instead of racing ahead
- Read-aloud keeps playing when you minimize the app or the screen turns off
- The media player and lock screen now show the story's picture, or the CyPress logo when it has none
- The widget can show up to six stories, with the source name on each
- Fixed a bug in the new read-aloud code that could have turned read-aloud off on the phone

1.13
- Sync is safer: it now merges edits from two devices instead of one overwriting the other, and a deleted story, feed or folder stays deleted
- Backup and restore are safer: Start fresh makes a backup first and asks three times, restoring a backup replaces everything cleanly, and backups no longer contain your sync code
- Read-aloud keeps playing between stories, pauses for phone calls and other audio, and stops when you swipe the app away
- Alerts, quiet hours and digest notices are now saved properly for the background checker, which only runs when the battery isn’t low (and on Wi-Fi when Data saver is on)
- The Daily and big libraries are faster, and saving is gentler on storage
- Links from feeds are checked so only web pages open, and prompts are now CyPress dialogs instead of system pop-ups
- Feeds are more forgiving: odd date formats, non-UTF-8 sites, RDF feeds and missing links now load, and a feed that’s gone (404) stops quickly
- Start-up errors show a recovery screen with a link to the latest version, and Diagnostics now includes a short error log
- Cyber and Aurora themes use less battery, and background effects pause while you read

1.14
- Share now sends a link that opens a page showing the story, with a button to read it and a way to get CyPress; you can still share the story card as a picture
- Every dropdown in the app now opens a CyPress picker instead of the phone’s plain list; the font pickers show each name in its own typeface
- The font picker now has 10 fonts in each group (Serif, Sans, Easy reading, Typewriter and mono), 19 of them new
- The story menu is shorter and grouped, with Share… asking whether to share a link or a picture
- Aurora is now a thin line of soft colour along the top of the screen instead of full-screen lights, so scrolling stays smooth
- Theme names fit on the Fold, The Daily title lines up with the CyPress logo, and the Dynamic look box is no longer cut off

1.15
- Share is now one tap and always sends a link that opens a page showing the story, with a button to read it and a way to get CyPress; the picture option is gone because it never worked reliably
- Share links now use the cypressreader.com address, and no personal GitHub address is left inside the app
- CyPress now also runs on iPhone as a web app at cypressreader.com/app
- The “couldn’t start” recovery screen now points to cypressreader.com for the latest version

1.16
- New app icon: the CyPress tree now fills the whole icon on dark navy, matching the website, with no green border
- The circuit icon you can pick in Settings matches the new look
- The iPhone web app icon matches too

1.17
- New Newsroom layout on laptops and big screens: feeds open with a lead story, a Latest list and a mix of photo blocks, pull quotes and headline lists that change from feed to feed and day to day
- The Daily fills the wide screen too, with a bigger Trending block, sections that alternate layouts, and more to scroll
- Smooth mode in Settings turns off blurs and loads stories as you scroll. It is on by default in Safari to fix choppy scrolling and slow feed switching
- Switching to another feed now starts at the top, and the feed title no longer flashes while the cover loads
- The 3D tree and circuit icons match the new full-bleed icon, and the sidebar rows are tighter

1.18
- Feeds on phones and tablets now get the same varied magazine layout as big screens: a tall lead story, headline lists, side-by-side pairs, a swipeable photo strip, colour features and pull quotes, arranged differently for each feed and day
- The new layout now covers tablets held upright too. Choose Compact or Narrow cards in Settings to keep the classic grid
- Fixed the Latest list looking cut off on the left, and the dark bars behind some headlines
- Fixed small page jumps when you tap a star or a story is redrawn

1.19
- The Daily on phones now has the varied magazine look, with a Trending feature, sections in different shapes and a long-reads strip
- Play my Daily reads full stories aloud and skips subscriber-only sites. Settings is now six clear pages with a search box and fewer groups
- Photos are cropped around the main subject, so faces and focal points stay in view
- Smoother launch: no more colour flash or odd loading screen when the app opens, and feeds draw and switch faster
- A new Caught up card appears when you reach the end of a feed
- Android 13+ themed icon: the tree and circuit icons tint to your wallpaper
- The Daily editor's note reads like a proper letter, with a closing and signature, and no longer repeats itself

1.20
- Each site's feed now opens with a front-page header: its name, badge, tagline, address and quick stats, with Mute for a week
- The Daily shows how much of today's issue you have read, with a progress ring and a small ring on each section
- The app keeps your place when you come back after leaving it, instead of jumping to the top
- Smoother scrolling: photo analysis now waits until you stop, and background upkeep no longer runs while you scroll
- The small i button on a story now only appears when it has something to tell you, like several of your sites covering it
- Cover title style is now Auto by default: each cover picks the look that reads best on its photo. The five styles that stay are Clean, Tinted, Glow, Gradient and Elegant italic; Outline, Spaced caps and Bold shadow were removed
- Shared links are shorter, because the story excerpt is no longer packed into the link
- Story text on phones is now left-aligned, which fixes the big gaps between words and the stretched underlines on links
- Stories no longer show hidden screen-reader labels such as "list 1 of 3" and "end of list" inside lists, and bullet lists are never stretched
- Folder names in the side menu on phones and tablets are no longer cut off after a few letters. Press and hold a folder or feed to delete it
- On phones the feed menu now floats over most of the screen with the stories dimmed behind it, and closes when you pick a feed, so names have room to breathe
- Fixed several automated checks that had gone out of date

1.21
- Share links can now be short, like cypressreader.com/s/k3F9aQz. This needs a one-time setup of the share worker on Cloudflare; until then links stay as long as before

1.22
- Scrolling is much smoother: the photo-analysis step used to freeze the screen for a moment while it decoded full-size photos, and now does that work in the background
- Smooth mode is now on by default on phones and tablets (it skips blurs and soft shadows). You can turn it off in Settings if you prefer the softer look
- New in Settings, About and help: Check scrolling. It records how smooth scrolling is on your device for about 15 seconds and copies a report you can send to the developer

1.23
- Fixed the stutter while scrolling that your Check scrolling report pinpointed: refreshing feeds in the background used to redraw the page and save a large cache while you were scrolling
- When new stories arrive and you are scrolled down, a small "New stories. Tap to show" bar appears instead of redrawing under your finger. The page refreshes by itself when you scroll back to the top
- Reading feeds and saving stories now wait until you stop scrolling

1.24
- Check scrolling now records what the app was doing around each slow moment (which job, how long, memory use), so rare stalls can be traced to their cause. Run it again and send the report

1.25
- Removed a pause of up to a quarter of a second that happened when you touched the screen after leaving CyPress idle for a while: the page used to be re-styled in full each time the background effects woke up
- Your last Check scrolling report was clean: 96% of frames under 20 ms. The remaining stalls were that wake-up and switching feeds

1.26
- Opening a story is faster: the page is now laid out twice instead of four times
- Leaving a story no longer redraws the whole feed behind it when you only read. It is instant and keeps your place
- Smoother scrolling while CyPress loads in the background: the little loading logo used to make the browser restyle the page over a hundred times; it now updates a few times a second and never while you scroll
- Opening Settings no longer fetches reading fonts up front, only when you open the Reading page
- The side menu is only redrawn when it actually changes, and the redraw after a refresh does less work
- Easier to read themes: loud display faces (Bangers, Uncial, Bebas, Righteous, Orbitron, VT323 and others) are replaced by clear ones, the pixel theme no longer sets article text in a pixel font, and text rows can no longer have a theme border run through the words

1.27
- Front page cover captions in the small boxes are larger, sentence case and no longer show a sliver of a third line; the edition line no longer runs under the info button
- Techmeme removed from the suggested feeds (it lists headline links, not stories)
- Spotlight and Broadcast front pages: the CyPress name no longer disappears with the tint or gradient masthead styles, and Broadcast shows more of the photo with a smaller headline
- Title-sequence front page: the headline now sits at the bottom and is smaller, so it no longer covers the face in the photo
- Index front page fits on one screen without scrolling
- Journal front page no longer shows the same photo twice, and fits more of the headline
- Gazette, Gallery, Index, Journal and similar front pages: the CyPress name is dark on light paper again, no longer pale and hard to read with the tint, gradient or glow masthead styles
- Switching a Quick look no longer plays the sunrise or sunset animation in the corner, and the CyPress name in the style preview stays on one line
- Dynamic theme now reads in Literata, a typeface designed for long reading on screens, at a medium weight so headlines are clear without being heavy
- Folding or unfolding your phone, or rotating it, keeps your place in the story you are reading instead of jumping back to the start
- Story text no longer opens wide gaps between words when the column is narrow or the text size is large: it left-aligns unless the line is long enough to justify cleanly
- Daily list rows on wide screens give the headline more room: smaller photo column and a lighter headline weight, so titles no longer wrap into tall stacks

1.28
- Opening your phone no longer sends a story you were reading in scroll view back to the beginning: your place carries over to the page view, and back
- Dynamic theme now uses Inter, a modern sans designed for screens, in place of the serif
- Closing your fold while a story is being read aloud keeps your place and no longer snaps you back: the place carries over even while photos finish loading, and scrolling by hand is not fought by the read-aloud follow for a few seconds
- Feed headlines: every theme now uses a clear, modern face that still suits it (the old display faces such as Bangers, Bebas and Uncial are gone from the feeds), and feed headlines are a lighter weight. Story text fonts are unchanged
- Feed photos: lazy-loaded and srcset images are now found, and Google icons and tiny tracking pixels are never used as a story photo. Two dead fallback proxies removed so a failing feed gives up sooner
- CNN video pages and similar no longer show the video-feedback form or a column of menu links as the story text
- Clearer problems: a feed that isn’t loading now says why (offline, blocked, moved, slow or unreadable) and when it last updated, and the Add feed box explains what went wrong and that nothing was added
- Themes: a short Featured row comes first, and the other 48 styles sit under More styles so nobody has to scroll through them all
- Sync and privacy wording is plain now: no account needed, your data stays yours, and the private relay is explained as optional
- Settings now say whether you are ready for offline reading: how many stories are stored and when your feeds last updated
