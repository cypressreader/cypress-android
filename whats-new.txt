CyPress v1.2
Eight new themes (Blueprint, E-Ink, Lavender, Alpenglow, Solar Flare, Sage, Amber CRT, Outrun), sorted into groups.
Sepia gets Parchment and Vellum papers. Newsprint gets an optional grain.
Author and site notes: bios and site blurbs at the start or end of a story fold into a small note you can open.
Match theme font is now the default, so each theme brings its own fonts.
Cleaner phone reading with more room for the story, and crisper headlines on cards.
Back closes just the story instead of leaving the app.
Removed the ticker, photo essay, Define and Translate. Links open normally.
