Get CyPress

Free on Android. On iPhone and iPad it installs from Safari in about 20 seconds.

iPhone and iPad

There is no App Store download. CyPress is a web app: add it to your Home Screen once and it opens full-screen like any other app.

  1. 1
    Open this page in Safari.Not inside Instagram, Facebook, Mail or another app’s built-in browser. Go to cypressreader.com/app, or copy that address into Safari.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share button The square with an arrow pointing up. It is in the bar at the bottom of the screen (on iPad it is at the top). If you do not see the bar, tap the bottom edge of the screen once.
  3. 3
    Choose “Add to Home Screen” Scroll down in the sheet that opens until you see it. If it is not there, tap “View More” first. Then tap Add in the top corner.

Done. Open CyPress from the new icon on your Home Screen. Your feeds and settings stay on your phone, and it works without an account. Read-aloud may pause when you lock the screen, because iOS limits web apps; the Android app does not have that limit. If you clear Safari’s website data, CyPress will start fresh.

Android

Download the app directly. It takes three taps.

  1. 1
    Tap the button below.Your phone downloads a file called cypress.apk.
  2. 2
    Open the file.If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. 3
    Tap Install, then open CyPress.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no account.

Where is my data?

Your feeds, settings and saved stories stay on your own device. CyPress has no server that stores them.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

On iPhone it is a web app, so you add it from Safari instead (steps above). On Android you install the app file directly.

Does it work offline?

Stories you have already loaded can be read again without a connection. New stories need internet.

I don’t see “Add to Home Screen”

Make sure you are in Safari itself, not an app’s built-in browser. Tap Share, scroll to the bottom of the sheet and tap “View More” or “Edit Actions” if the row is hidden.

Can I add any website?

Any site with a news feed (RSS). Search by name or paste the address, and CyPress finds the feed.

Make your news look like it matters

Pick your sites. CyPress does the layout.
