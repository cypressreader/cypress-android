How it works

Pick your sites. We do the layout.

CyPress Reader is a news reader. You choose where the news comes from, and it turns those sites into something you can sit down with.

  1. No. I

    Add the sites you like

    Search by name or paste an address. There is a ready-made catalogue to start from.

  2. No. II

    Read your front page

    The Daily gathers today’s stories from every site into one magazine-style page, with the biggest story at the top.

  3. No. III

    Get to the end

    Once you have caught up, CyPress Reader says so. There is no endless scroll to get lost in.

Read aloud

Let the news read to you.

Tap Play Daily and CyPress Reader reads the day’s stories one after another, like a podcast, using the voices built into your phone. On Android it keeps playing with the screen off, and you can pause or skip from your lock screen or your Bluetooth buttons.

Get started

Get CyPress Reader

On Android you install the app directly. On iPhone and iPad it runs from Safari, and installs to your Home Screen in about 20 seconds.

Android

Download the app file directly. It takes three taps.

  1. 1
    Tap the button below.Your phone downloads a file called cypress.apk. It is the app itself, and it is not from the Play Store.
  2. 2
    Open the file.If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source. If Play Protect offers to scan the app, choose Scan app. That is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  3. 3
    Tap Install, then open CyPress Reader.

iPhone and iPad

There is no App Store download. CyPress Reader is a web app: add it to your Home Screen once and it opens full-screen like any other app.

  1. 1
    Open this page in Safari.Use Safari itself, not an in-app browser. Go to cypressreader.com/app.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share button.The square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari’s bar.
  3. 3
    Choose “Add to Home Screen”.Scroll down until you see it (tap “View More” if needed), then tap Add.

Done. Open CyPress Reader from the new icon on your Home Screen. Your sites and settings stay on your phone. Read-aloud may pause when you lock the screen, because iOS limits web apps; the Android app does not have that limit.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Open CyPress Reader and start adding sites. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data?

Your sites, settings and saved stories stay on your own device. CyPress Reader has no server that stores them. One thing to know: in the web version, when CyPress Reader loads a site’s news feed or a full article, the request may pass through a relay service so the browser is allowed to read it. The Android app fetches directly, and in Settings you can point the web version at a relay of your own. The privacy page lists every service it can talk to.

Why isn’t it in an app store?

On iPhone it is a web app, so you add it from Safari instead (steps above). On Android you install the app file directly.

Does it work offline?

Stories you have already loaded can be read again without a connection. New stories need the internet.

Can I add any website?

Almost any website. Search by name or paste the address, and CyPress Reader finds its news feed.

I don’t see “Add to Home Screen”

Make sure you are in Safari itself, not an app’s built-in browser. Tap Share, scroll to the bottom of the sheet and tap “View More” or “Edit Actions” if the row is hidden.
