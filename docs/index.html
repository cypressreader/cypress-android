CyPress

The news you follow, laid out like a magazine.

  • A front page built from today’s stories, with big photos and real headlines
  • Read-aloud that keeps playing with the screen off
  • Dozens of themes, from newsprint to neon
  • Add any site you like. No account needed
Get CyPress for AndroidOpen on iPhone

On iPhone: open this in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. CyPress then opens full-screen like an app.

How to install
  1. Tap the button. Your phone downloads a file called cypress.apk.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Tap Install, then open CyPress.

On iPhone

CyPress runs in Safari and installs to your Home Screen. It opens full-screen, like an app.

  1. Open cypressreader.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open CyPress from your Home Screen. Your feeds stay on your phone.
